Elastic FY27 sales process

Map opportunities to MEDDPICC stage

Copy a prompt into Claude Desktop after install. DCOS pulls live Salesforce data and indexed notes, then maps each opportunity to Elastic's 8-stage process with MEDDPICC maturity scoring — grounded in CRM truth, not guesswork.

Requires: Digital Chief of Staff + Jina connectors enabled under Connectors → Desktop. Run dcos_sfdc_sync before territory-wide reviews.

8-stage quick reference

StageNameForecastKey question
1QualificationOmittedIs this opportunity real? (3-Whys)
2DiscoveryPipelineWhat do they need, and can we reach the EB?
3EB AlignmentUpsideDoes the EB agree? (8x criteria)
4EngagementUpside / Strong UpsideIs Elastic the preferred business solution?
5ValidationStrong Upside / CommitDoes Elastic technically work? (Tech + Migration win)
6Proposal & NegotiationCommitCan we agree on commercial terms?
7Pending ClosureCommitOrder validated internally?
8CloseBookedCustomer set up for success?

Prompts

Map one opp to the correct Elastic stage, score MEDDPICC vs expected maturity, and list exit-criteria gaps.

Claude prompt — opportunity stage map

Run dcos_user_profile, then dcos_sfdc_sync.

Map this opportunity to Elastic's FY27 8-stage sales process and score MEDDPICC maturity.

Opportunity: [OPPORTUNITY NAME or SFDC ID]
Account: [ACCOUNT NAME]

DATA (use tools — never invent):
1. dcos_sfdc_get_opportunity — stage, ACV, close date, owner, next step, key fields
2. dcos_sfdc_get_account — account context
3. dcos_search — prior MEDDPICC notes, MAP, BVR, champion/EB mentions (2–3 queries)
4. dcos_sfdc_query — recent Tasks/Events if activity context is thin

AUTHORITATIVE STAGE MODEL:
| Stg | Name | Forecast | Primary win / gate |
|-----|------|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Qualification | Omitted | 3-Whys answered; pain + compelling event; path to EB |
| 2 | Discovery | Pipeline | MAP v1 shared; champion tested; metrics quantifying |
| 3 | EB Alignment | Upside | Direct EB meeting; 8x criteria validated; MAP agreed |
| 4 | Engagement | Upside/Strong | Business Win — Elastic preferred; BVR with EB |
| 5 | Validation | Strong/Commit | Technical Win + Migration Win; MAP complete; SOW in progress |
| 6 | Proposal & Negotiation | Commit | Signed Order Form or PO |
| 7 | Pending Closure | Commit | Order Ops validated |
| 8 | Close | Booked | Handoff to CA / services complete |

MEDDPICC EXPECTED MATURITY BY STAGE (compare actual vs expected):
| Element | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Metrics | Identify | Identify & Quantify | Validate w/ EB | Expand (BVR) | Validate in Proof | Sustained |
| Economic Buyer | Identify | Path mapped | Aligned (8x) | Routine touchpoints | Confirm sponsorship | Sustained |
| Decision Criteria | — | Initial | In MAP | Expanding | Achieved (tech win) | Sustained |
| Decision Process | — | — | In MAP | Executing MAP | MAP complete | Sustained |
| Paper Process | — | — | Begin if >$250K | Initial discussions | Mapped; redlines begun | Executing |
| Identify Pain | Identify | Quantify | Validate w/ EB | Quantify (BVR) | Demonstrate in PoC | Sustained |
| Champion | Identify | Test | Validated (EB mtg) | Active | Sustained | Sustained |
| Competition | Identify | Identify | Battle card | Address w/ EB | Watch FUD | Active defense |

SCORING: For each MEDDPICC letter score 0–3:
0 = no evidence in CRM/notes | 1 = identified only | 2 = developing / partial | 3 = at or above expected maturity for recommended stage

ELASTIC-SPECIFIC CHECKS:
- Translate technical metrics to business language the EB cares about
- Distinguish Coach vs Champion (will they sell Elastic when you're not in the room?)
- Flag if EB for $350K+ deal is different from practitioner who approved smaller spend
- Winning the POC is not enough — business case and EB alignment must lead technical eval

STAGE 3 — 8x EB CRITERIA (score each: Confirmed / Partial / Unknown / Gap):
1. Pain worth EB time and budget
2. Business & technical outcomes (EB vision of success)
3. Budget (planned, amount, CAPEX/OPEX)
4. Timeline (deadline + budget availability)
5. Decision Criteria
6. Decision Process
7. Services (implementation owner, services budgeted)
8. Partners (transact via partner, tie to bigger initiative)

STAGES 1–2 — 3-WHYS:
- Why Do Anything? (compelling event + timeline)
- Why Elastic? (differentiation vs alternatives)
- Why Now? (deadline forcing decision)

OUTPUT FORMAT:
## Recommended Elastic stage (1–8) + confidence (High/Med/Low)
## SFDC stage vs Elastic stage — explain any gap
## Forecast category that matches recommended stage
## MEDDPICC scorecard
| Letter | Score 0–3 | Expected at stage | Actual maturity | Evidence (CRM/notes only) | Gap |
## 3-Whys or 8x EB criteria (whichever applies)
## Exit criteria checklist — what is missing to advance one stage
## Stuck signals — match FY27 patterns for this stage; cite evidence
## Risk summary — top 3 deal risks by weakest MEDDPICC letters
## Next 3 actions — specific, owner, target date
## Manager coaching questions (3–5)

After analysis, call dcos_add_note with a concise MEDDPICC stage snapshot for future delta comparison.

For AVPs and managers — rank open opps by stage misalignment and MEDDPICC risk.

Claude prompt — territory MEDDPICC sweep

Run dcos_user_profile, then dcos_sfdc_sync.

Produce a territory MEDDPICC stage map for my open pipeline this quarter.

1. dcos_sfdc_list_opportunities — all open opps in my territory (or filter: closing this quarter)
2. For each opp in the top 15 by ACV: dcos_search for existing MEDDPICC notes (one query per opp if needed)

For EACH opportunity output a one-line row:
| Account | Opp | ACV | SFDC Stage | Recommended Elastic Stage (1–8) | Weakest MEDDPICC letter | Stage misalignment? (Y/N) | 🔴/🟡/🟢 |

Then deep-dive the top 5 🔴 deals using the full single-opportunity format (recommended stage, MEDDPICC scorecard, exit gaps, next action).

ROLLUP SECTIONS:
## Territory heat map — count of opps by recommended Elastic stage
## MEDDPICC weakness rollup — which letters are weakest across the patch (M, E, D, D, P, I, C, C)
## Stuck deals — opps in Stage 2 >4 weeks without EB plan, Stage 4+ with silent EB, Stage 5+ without legal started
## Rep coaching themes — 3 patterns you see across deals (not generic advice)
## One leadership action for my next 1:1 block

Rules: never invent ACV, stage, or close dates. Flag opps with insufficient CRM data separately.

When a deal feels active but isn't advancing — regression signals and recovery plan.

Claude prompt — stuck deal diagnostic

Run dcos_user_profile, then dcos_sfdc_sync.

This deal feels stuck. Diagnose against Elastic FY27 stage regression and stuck-deal signals.

Opportunity: [OPPORTUNITY NAME]
Account: [ACCOUNT NAME]
Seller concern: [e.g. "been in discovery 6 weeks, no EB meeting"]

1. dcos_sfdc_get_opportunity + dcos_search (notes, activities, prior briefs)
2. dcos_sfdc_query — Tasks/Events in last 90 days (meeting cadence, EB attendance)

DIAGNOSE:
A) Recommended Elastic stage today (1–8) with evidence
B) Has the deal REGRESSED? Check triggers:
   - EB departure/change → regress to Stage 2
   - Champion loss/disengagement → regress to Stage 2
   - Budget pull → regress to Stage 2 or 3
   - Scope change invalidating tech win → regress to Stage 4
   - Decision process reset → regress to Stage 3
   - Competitor re-entry → regress to Stage 4

C) Match STUCK SIGNALS for current stage:
   Stage 1: one contact only, vague pain, no compelling event, can't answer 3-Whys
   Stage 2: 4+ weeks no EB plan, no MAP shared, discovery loops
   Stage 3: Champion delays EB, superficial EB meeting, 8x gaps on Budget/Timeline
   Stage 4: silent EB, endless demos, Business Win not explicit
   Stage 5: scope creep, no eval completion date, legal not started (>$250K)
   Stage 6: redlines >2 weeks, surprise procurement, new approvers

OUTPUT:
## Verdict — Stuck / Regressed / Correct stage but under-developed MEDDPICC
## Root cause (1 paragraph, evidence-based)
## Recommended stage after honest assessment (include regression if warranted)
## Recovery plan — 5 specific actions with dates
## What to tell the manager in 60 seconds
## dcos_add_note — save diagnosis summary
