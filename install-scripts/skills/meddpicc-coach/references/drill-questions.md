# Drill Questions — sharpest opener per MEDDPICC element

Use one of these to open a Socratic drill. Stay focused — don't fire all of them.

## Metrics

- "What's the dollar value to *them* if this works? Show your math."
- "Who quantified that number — the rep, the champion, or the EB? In what words?"
- "If they don't buy anything, what does inaction cost them in 12 months?"
- "Have you tied savings or revenue to a specific budget line they already own?"

## Economic Buyer

- "Name the EB. Have you met them? In what setting? What did they say back?"
- "What's their approval limit for this deal size — and how do you know?"
- "What do they personally lose if this deal closes wrong?"
- "If the EB walked away tomorrow, would the deal die or just slow down?"

## Decision Criteria

- "Show me the written criteria. If there isn't one, who's deciding what 'good' looks like?"
- "Which criterion are you weakest on — and who said it matters?"
- "Did you help shape these criteria, or are you reacting to a list someone else wrote?"
- "Is there a vendor scorecard? Have you seen it? Have you reviewed your row?"

## Decision Process

- "Walk me through the next 6 steps to signature, with names and dates."
- "Who has veto power that you haven't met yet?"
- "What's the board meeting / budget cycle / fiscal milestone driving the date?"
- "Has anyone on their side run this exact procurement before? Where did it stall?"

## Paper Process

- "When did legal first see the contract? Security? Procurement?"
- "What's their average cycle time for a deal this size — and how do you know?"
- "Who signs? Manual or DocuSign? Does the EB even have authority on this paper?"
- "What's the riskiest redline you're going to hit, and who fights it?"

## Identify Pain

- "What is the compelling event — with a date and a consequence?"
- "If they delay 90 days, what specifically breaks?"
- "Who feels the pain personally? Name them and the words they used."
- "Why now and not 6 months ago? Why not 6 months from now?"

## Champion

- "What has your champion done *inside* the company when you weren't in the room?"
- "What do they personally gain — promotion, project win, headache solved?"
- "Have they introduced you to power, or just to peers?"
- "If your champion got hit by a bus tomorrow, does this deal survive?"

## Competition

- "Name the alternatives — including 'do nothing' and 'build in-house.'"
- "Why us over each one, in their words — not yours?"
- "Where are you weakest vs. the front-runner? How do you neutralize?"
- "If price were equal, which way do they go? Why?"

## How to push back when a rep gives a vague answer

| Rep says | You ask |
|---|---|
| "They love us" | "What have they done to prove it?" |
| "End of Q2" | "Said by whom, in what context, what's the consequence if missed?" |
| "Probably the CFO" | "Have you met them? What did they say?" |
| "They want to save time" | "Hours per week × wage × frequency. Show me the math." |
| "We're the front-runner" | "Who told you that, and what would they need to see to stay there?" |
| "They've used us before" | "Different champion, different EB, different criteria — what carries over?" |
| "Sarah's our champion" | "What has Sarah done internally to advance this when you weren't there?" |
